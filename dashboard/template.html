Data: real NCBI records distributed with the Biopython 1.85 and Pyrodigal 3.7.1 test suites. A call counts as correct when strand and stop codon match a single-exon annotated CDS. Multi-exon genes, pseudogenes and genes spanning the sequence origin are neutral and excluded from precision. With 70 evaluable chloroplast genes, F1 intervals overlap between most methods.
